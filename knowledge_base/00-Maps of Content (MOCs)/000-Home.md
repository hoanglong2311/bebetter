# Welcome to Your Backend Learning Vault! 🚀

This vault is your dedicated space for learning backend engineering principles, exploring technologies, and dissecting real-world codebase examples to prepare for your mid-level backend engineer interviews.

**Purpose:** To systematically build a comprehensive knowledge base from the `cellutions` codebase and relevant backend engineering concepts, optimized for review and interview readiness.

**Navigation:**

This vault is structured using **Maps of Content (MOCs)** to guide your learning journey. Each MOC acts as a central hub for a major section of your knowledge base.

Explore the main areas of your vault through these MOCs:

*   **[[010-Core Concepts MOC|Core Concepts]]**:  Dive into fundamental backend principles like distributed systems, microservices, and event-driven architectures. Build a strong theoretical foundation.
*   **[[020-Technologies MOC|Technologies]]**:  Deepen your understanding of specific technologies relevant to backend engineering and the `cellutions` stack, including Redis, Cassandra, Pulsar, Flink, Kubernetes, and Golang.
*   **[[030-Repositories MOC|Repositories]]**:  Explore the `cellutions` codebase in a structured way. Analyze individual repositories, understand their architecture, and learn from real-world code examples.
*   **[[040-Projects MOC|Projects]]**:  Document your personal projects, coding exercises, and system design implementations. Practice applying your learned knowledge in practical scenarios.
*   **[[050-Interview Prep MOC|Interview Preparation]]**:  Focus your efforts on interview-specific topics, including system design questions, coding challenges, and behavioral interview strategies.
*   **[[060-Cheat Sheets MOC|Cheat Sheets]]**:  Quickly access cheat sheets and reference notes for commands, syntax, and key concepts.

**Using the Graph View:**

Obsidian's **Graph View** is your superpower for navigating this vault!

1.  **Open Graph View:**  Use `Ctrl+Shift+G` (or `Cmd+Shift+G` on Mac) to open the Graph View.
2.  **Observe Central Hub:** You should see this **`000-Home` note as a central node** in your graph. This is because it links to all the main MOC notes.
3.  **Explore Connections:**  Click and drag nodes in the graph to rearrange them. Hover over nodes to see note titles.
4.  **Follow the Links:** Click on the `000-Home` node or any MOC node and then click on linked nodes to navigate to different sections of your knowledge base.
5.  **Identify Clusters:**  Notice how notes related to specific topics (e.g., Redis, Microservices, a particular repository) form clusters in the graph. This visually represents related concepts.
6.  **Use Filters (Optional):**
    *   **Tags:** If you've used tags (e.g., `#redis`, `#microservice`), you can filter the graph view to only show notes with specific tags.  This can help focus on particular technology areas.
    *   **Path:** Filter by path to focus on notes within a specific folder (e.g., `20-Technologies/Redis/`).
